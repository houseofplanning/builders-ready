Want to get paid through the app? Connect Stripe 💳

This bit’s optional, but it’s the one that pays for itself. Connect Stripe and your clients can pay milestone invoices by card or bank, straight to your own account — money lands the day a stage is signed off, no chasing transfers. (You can skip it and still mark invoices paid manually or in cash.)

On the web (it’s not in the app): go to Settings → Billing and tap “Set up Stripe Express.” Stripe walks you through verifying your details and bank account — takes about five minutes.

A quick run-through of the app 🔨

When you open the app, your home screen has everything front and centre — you don’t need a project to get going:

1. Marketplace — new jobs from local homeowners land here. Open one, send a quote. No per-lead fee, it’s included.
2. Quotes & estimates — build a quote on site from your costs and margin, send a branded PDF the client signs on their phone.
3. Win it → it’s a project — an accepted quote becomes a managed job: timeline, site photos, signed variations and invoices, all in one place.
4. Get paid & hand over — raise staged invoices, get paid in the app, and finish with a one-tap handover PDF for the client.
