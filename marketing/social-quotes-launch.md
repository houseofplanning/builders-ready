# Social launch — Quotes & Estimates (@BuildersReadyUK)

For Instagram Reels + TikTok. Speaks to builders (win more jobs) and lands for anyone new to Builders Ready.

---

## The hook (first 2 seconds — this is everything)

Open on a phone held up in a half-finished room / on a doorstep, text on screen:

> **"You're stood in the client's kitchen. Quote the whole job before you leave."**

If using a Zeely avatar: same line delivered to camera, confident, quick.

---

## Short video script (~20–25s, screen-record + voiceover)

**[0–3s] Hook.** Phone in hand on site.
VO: *"Most builders quote from the van that night. By then, someone else already has."*

**[3–8s] Screen-record: adding lines.** Tapping in labour days, materials, a saved rate.
VO: *"Build it here, on the spot — materials, labour, your margin."*
On-screen text: *"Enter your cost + markup → client price is worked out for you"*

**[8–14s] The total ticking up, then the branded quote / PDF.**
VO: *"Watch the total add up. Send it as a proper branded quote before you've left the drive."*

**[14–20s] Client taps 'Accept' on the shared link → 'Accepted'.**
VO: *"They accept and sign on their phone…"*

**[20–25s] Quote → Project.**
VO: *"…and it turns straight into a job. That's the builder they remember."*
End card: **BUILDERS READY** wordmark + *"Free for 14 days · buildersready.uk"*

## Notes for posting

- Lead with the on-screen hook text within the first second — most viewers decide there.
- The strongest 6 seconds are the screen-record of the total ticking up and the "Accepted" tap; keep those tight and readable.
- Post the same cut to TikTok and Instagram Reels; on TikTok, a plain-spoken talking-head intro before the screen-record tends to hold longer.
- If you can film one real quote being built on an actual doorstep, that beats any avatar — authenticity converts here.
