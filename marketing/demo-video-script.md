# Builders Ready — Demo Video Script & Shot List

**Approach: film once, cut twice.** Record the clips in the shot list, then assemble a short vertical cut for Instagram/TikTok and a longer version for the website.

---

## 1. Shot list — record these clips once

Record each as a short, clean clip. Screen-record the **web** on your laptop and the **client app** on your iPhone (Settings → Control Centre → Screen Recording).

**Web (dashboard):**
- **A — Dashboard overview:** the colourful dashboard (greeting, KPI tiles, cash-position chart, ring). Slow scroll top to bottom.
- **B — Open a project:** click into a project → the project page with the finance chart, timeline and sections.
- **C — Raise a decision:** create a decision with 2 options + prices.
- **D — Propose a variation:** fill in a variation (title, cost, days).
- **E — Handover PDF:** click generate → the branded PDF appears (show the cover page).

**Mobile (iPhone client app):**
- **F — Branded app home / timeline:** the client's project view with progress photos.
- **G — Push notification:** a decision notification arriving (or the Decisions tab).
- **H — Approve a decision:** client taps an option to approve it.
- **I — Sign a variation:** client signs with their finger.
- **J — Invoices / handover:** the invoices tab and/or the handover PDF on the phone.

**Hero shot:**
- **K — Phone in hand:** the app open on your phone, held in hand or dropped into a phone frame in editing.

> Tip: use the demo tenant (Heritage Build Co) so everything's populated. Keep each clip 3–6 seconds of clean action.

## 2. Cut A — Instagram / TikTok (vertical 9:16, ~25 seconds)

Fast cuts, bold on-screen captions (most people watch muted), upbeat music. The first 2 seconds must hook.

| Time | Visual | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | **K** — app on phone, punch-in | **Your clients have never seen a builder do this** |
| 0:02–0:05 | **F** — app timeline | Give them their own app for the job |
| 0:05–0:08 | **H** — client approves a decision | They approve choices on their phone |
| 0:08–0:11 | **I** — signing a variation | Extra costs signed off *before* the work |
| 0:11–0:14 | **J / F** — invoices + progress photos | Invoices + progress, all in one place |
| 0:14–0:17 | **E** — handover PDF | One handover document at the end |
| 0:17–0:20 | **A** — web dashboard scroll | You run it all from one dashboard |
| 0:20–0:25 | **K** — app + logo | **Builders Ready · from £29/mo**<br>First 10 builders → 3 months free<br>buildersready.uk |

**Notes:** no slow logo intro — open on the hook. Cut every 2–3s. Add a subtle zoom/punch-in on each shot. Keep captions centred and out of the bottom ~15% (that's where TikTok/Reels buttons sit).

## 3. Cut B — Website (16:9 or 9:16, ~75 seconds)

Calmer, explanatory. Record a voiceover (your own voice, or ElevenLabs) and keep captions on for muted autoplay.

**Voiceover script (with matching visuals):**

1. *"Right now, most builders run their jobs from a WhatsApp group. Messages get lost, variations turn into arguments, and the client never really knows what's happening."*
   — Visual: **F** app home (as the calm contrast), or a quick messy-chat shot.

2. *"Builders Ready changes that. You give every client their own branded app for their project."*
   — Visual: **K / F** branded app.

3. *"They see a live timeline, with progress photos straight from site."*
   — Visual: **F** timeline + photos.

4. *"Decisions? They approve them on their phone — logged with the date and the price. No more 'we never agreed that'."*
   — Visual: **G → H** notification then approve.

5. *"Variations get signed off before the work happens — legally clear, on their phone."*
   — Visual: **I** signing.

6. *"Invoices, all in one place. And at the end, a complete handover PDF they keep forever."*
   — Visual: **J** invoices + **E** handover PDF cover.

7. *"You run everything from one dashboard — every job's progress and money at a glance."*
   — Visual: **A / B** dashboard + cash chart.

8. *"Whether you're a sole trader or a multi-PM firm, it makes you look a step above the competition."*
   — Visual: **A** + **F** side by side (dashboard and app).

9. *"Builders Ready. From £29 a month, with a 14-day free trial. See it at buildersready.uk."*
   — Visual: logo + URL end card.

## 4. Production notes

- **Aspect ratios:** Social = 9:16 (1080×1920). Website = 16:9 (1920×1080), or a 9:16 if your hero section is mobile-first.
- **Edit in CapCut** (free, desktop + mobile): auto-captions, text overlays, music, device frames. It's built for this.
- **Phone footage:** for the website version, drop the iPhone recording into a phone frame (CapCut/Canva have them). For social, raw full-screen is fine.
- **Captions:** big, bold, high-contrast, sentence case. Keep them inside the safe zone (away from the bottom and right edges on social).
- **Music:** upbeat royalty-free bed for social; quieter bed under the voiceover for the website (music ~20% volume so the VO is clear). CapCut's library or Epidemic Sound.
- **Pace:** social cuts every 2–3s with punch-ins; website is smoother.
- **Export:** 1080p, MP4.
- **Hook rule (social):** the app + the bold claim must be on screen in the first 1–2 seconds. No intro card.

---

## 5. Where each goes

- **Instagram:** Reel (main feed reach) + you can trim a 15s version for Stories.
- **TikTok:** the same 9:16 file.
- **Website:** embed Cut B in the hero or a "See it in action" section, click-to-play with a clean poster frame (don't autoplay with sound).
