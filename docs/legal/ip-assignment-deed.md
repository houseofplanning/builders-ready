## Background

(A) The Assignor has created and/or owns certain intellectual property relating to the "Builders Ready" software application, platform and brand.

(B) The Assignor wishes to assign, and the Company wishes to take an assignment of, all such intellectual property on the terms of this Deed.

## Operative provisions

### 1. Definitions

**"Intellectual Property"** means all intellectual property rights of any kind, whether registered or unregistered and wherever in the world existing, relating to the Builders Ready application, platform and business, including without limitation:

(a) copyright and related rights in all source code, object code, software, scripts, configuration and documentation;
(b) the "Builders Ready" name, logo, brand, get-up and any associated goodwill, and any registered or unregistered trade marks and applications;
(c) design rights (registered and unregistered) in the user interface, graphics and related materials;
(d) database rights and rights in the structure and contents of any databases;
(e) the domain names **buildersready.uk** and **shtrades.co** and any other domains used in the business;
(f) rights in know-how, trade secrets and confidential information; and
(g) all other intellectual property rights, together with the right to sue for and recover damages for past infringements.

### 2. Assignment

In consideration of the sum of £1 paid by the Company to the Assignor (receipt of which the Assignor acknowledges), the Assignor assigns to the Company **absolutely and with full title guarantee** all of the Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Intellectual Property, to hold unto the Company absolutely, including the right to bring proceedings for and recover damages and other remedies in respect of any infringement occurring before the date of this Deed.

### 3. Present assignment of future rights

To the extent any of the Intellectual Property is not in existence or not owned by the Assignor at the date of this Deed, the Assignor assigns it to the Company by way of present assignment of future rights, to vest in the Company immediately upon its creation or acquisition.

### 4. Waiver of moral rights

The Assignor irrevocably waives all moral rights in the Intellectual Property to which the Assignor may be entitled under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 and any similar laws anywhere in the world.

### 5. Further assurance

The Assignor shall, at the Company's request and expense, promptly do all acts and execute all documents necessary to perfect the Company's title to the Intellectual Property, including assisting with the transfer or registration of any domain names, trade marks or other registrable rights into the Company's name.

### 6. Warranties

The Assignor warrants that: (a) the Assignor is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Intellectual Property; (b) the Intellectual Property is free from all encumbrances; and (c) so far as the Assignor is aware, the use of the Intellectual Property does not infringe the rights of any third party.

### 7. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Deed and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
