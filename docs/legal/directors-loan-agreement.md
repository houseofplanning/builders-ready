## Background

The Lender has agreed to make, and may from time to time make, loans to the Company to fund its working capital and operations. This Agreement records the terms on which those amounts are advanced.

## Terms

### 1. The Loan
The Lender agrees to lend to the Company such amounts as the Lender advances from time to time (each an "Advance", together the "Loan"). The parties anticipate regular advances of approximately £1,000 per month, but the amount and timing of each Advance are at the Lender's discretion.

### 2. Director's Loan Account
Each Advance will be credited to the Lender's director's loan account in the Company's books. The outstanding balance of the Loan at any time is the aggregate of all Advances less any repayments, as recorded in that account.

### 3. Interest
The Loan is **interest-free**.

### 4. Repayment
The Loan (or any part of it) is repayable to the Lender **on demand**, to the extent the Company has sufficient available funds to do so without prejudicing its ability to pay its other debts as they fall due. There is no fixed repayment date.

### 5. Security
The Loan is **unsecured**.

### 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
