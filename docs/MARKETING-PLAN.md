# Builders Ready — Marketing Plan (£800/month)

_Last updated: 1 Nov 2026. Owner: Oddie._
_Budget: ~£1,000/month director's loan, of which ~£200 is business upkeep and **~£800 is marketing**. This plan is built for that number: lean, measured, test-and-learn, and leaning hard on content Oddie can already produce (the TikTok/Reels/LinkedIn video engine + email list)._

---

## 1. What £800/month can and can't do

Be honest about the number. £800/month will **not** out-shout Tradify, Jobber or Powered Now on paid ads. What it *can* do is buy data: find the one or two channels where a trade signs up for less than they're worth, then pour the budget into those and starve the rest. The goal for the first 90 days is not growth, it's **learning your real cost to acquire a paying trade (CAC)**. Once that's known and under control, the same £800 scales predictably and the case for raising a bigger budget (or investment) writes itself.

## 2. Five strategic principles

1. **Trades first, homeowners later.** Trades pay the subscriptions, so every pound chases a paying trade. Homeowners are free and only valuable once an area has enough trades to answer their jobs. Don't run paid consumer campaigns yet, instead use "get homeowner leads from the Marketplace" as a *reason trades subscribe*.
2. **Concentrate geographically.** £800 spread across the UK is invisible. £800 focused on **one or two regions** builds visible density: local reviews, word of mouth, Marketplace liquidity, and Planning Radar actually having nearby jobs. Pick the area Oddie knows best / has the most existing users, and own it before widening.
3. **Organic-led, paid-amplified.** The content engine (video + email) is already built and nearly free. Paid money's job is to *amplify* what already works organically and to retarget people who've shown interest, not to carry cold reach on its own.
4. **One hook above all: Planning Radar.** "Builders Ready finds you building jobs before they're advertised" is a sharper, more native hook than "job management software". It's the thing competitors don't have. Lead with the win (more work), not the admin.
5. **Measure CAC against real LTV.** Once the founder dashboard is live (see `docs/SALE-READINESS.md`), use real ARPU and churn. Rule of thumb: aim for **CAC payback inside ~3-4 months** and **CAC under ~1/3 of LTV**. If a channel can't hit that, cut it.

## 3. The funnel and where money helps

| Stage | What's happening | Cheapest lever |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** | Trades discover Builders Ready | Organic video (free) + small paid prospecting tests |
| **Interest** | They click, watch, visit site | Retargeting (cheap, high ROI) + strong website (redesign in progress) |
| **Trial** | They sign up / start free | Clear CTA, the Flash48 offer, frictionless onboarding |
| **Paid** | Trial converts to subscription | Email nurture + in-app nudges (free) |
| **Retain/refer** | They stay and tell mates | Referral incentive + great product (free, highest ROI of all) |

Paid budget earns the most at **Interest (retargeting)** and **Trial (high-intent search)**. Reach is best fed organically.

## 4. The £800 monthly allocation (starting split — then follow the data)

| Channel | £/mo | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| **Meta ads (Facebook/Instagram)** — retargeting + small prospecting | **£300** | Tradespeople genuinely live in Facebook groups. Start with retargeting (cheap, converts) + a small lookalike/interest prospecting test. Boost the best-performing organic Reels here rather than making ad-only creative. |
| **Google Search ads** — tight, high-intent keywords only | **£250** | Catch people already looking: "builder quoting app", "job management software for builders", "[competitor] alternative". Small keyword set, exact/phrase match, UK + chosen region. High intent = best trial quality. |
| **Creator / content amplification** | **£150** | Pay a trusted trade creator for an honest post/collab, or put spend behind the single best organic video each month. One credible builder saying "this finds me work" beats any ad. |
| **Tools + experiments buffer** | **£100** | Scheduling tool, the odd design asset, a small budget to test one new thing a month (a trade newsletter sponsorship, a merchant leaflet, a local Facebook group promo). |

Rebalance monthly: whichever channel delivers the lowest CAC gets more next month; the worst gets cut. By month 3 this split will look different, and that's the point.

## 5. The content engine (near-free, do this regardless of budget)

This is the real engine; the £800 just amplifies it. Oddie already has the Remotion video setup and makes talking-head + app-demo videos.

- **Cadence:** aim for 3-5 short videos/week across TikTok, Instagram Reels and LinkedIn. Repurpose each cut to all three (house style already defined in the TikTok workspace `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Content pillars:** (1) **Planning Radar wins** ("your next job is already in the council's files"), (2) **quote-to-paid lifecycle** (branded quote the client signs from their inbox, straight into a project), (3) **builder pain/relief** (chasing invoices, admin, getting paid), (4) **proof** (real app screens, real numbers, before/after), (5) **light/engagement** (desk setup, day-in-the-life, no pitch).
- **Email:** the existing list is essentially free via Resend. A regular, useful email (a feature, a tip, a Planning Radar example) keeps trials converting and lapsed users warm. Keep running offer emails like Flash48 sparingly.
- **Blog/SEO:** low cost, slow burn. Target what trades search ("how to quote a job", "JCT contract for small builders", "finding building work"). Compounds over months.
- **App Store Optimisation:** free and high-leverage for a mobile app, title, keywords, screenshots, reviews. Ask happy users for App Store / Play reviews; ratings drive organic installs.

## 6. 90-day rollout

**Month 1 — set up to measure.**
- Finish the **founder dashboard** so CAC, trial starts, trial→paid and MRR are visible (it's the scoreboard for all of this).
- Stand up conversion tracking: Meta Pixel + Google tag + UTM tags on every link, so each signup is attributable to a channel.
- Launch small: Meta retargeting + a tight Google Search campaign, in the **chosen focus region**.
- Keep the organic video cadence going; tag which posts drive the most site visits.

**Month 2 — find the winner.**
- Read the numbers: which channel brings trials that actually convert and stick? CAC per channel.
- Double down on the best, cut the worst. Test one creator collaboration.
- Start/continue the email nurture for trials that didn't convert.

**Month 3 — concentrate and prove.**
- Put the majority of the £800 behind the single best channel in the single best region.
- Launch a **referral incentive** (e.g. a free month for the referrer and referee), word of mouth between trades is the cheapest acquisition there is.
- Write up what CAC/LTV looks like, this is the number that justifies a bigger budget or an investor conversation.

## 7. Metrics and targets (plug in real figures once the dashboard is live)

Track monthly, from the founder dashboard + ad platforms:
- **Spend per channel** and **cost per trial** and **CAC (cost per paying trade)**
- **Trial starts**, **trial→paid conversion %**, **new MRR**, **churned MRR**, **net MRR growth**
- **CAC payback** (months of subscription to recover CAC) — target < 3-4 months
- **LTV:CAC** — target > 3:1

Method for the affordable-CAC line (fill with real numbers): `LTV ≈ ARPU × gross margin × average months retained`; keep `CAC < LTV ÷ 3`. Until the dashboard gives real ARPU/churn, treat every channel as "on probation" and judge on cost per *converting* trial.

## 8. What NOT to spend on yet

- **Paid homeowner/consumer ads** — too expensive and premature until trade density exists in an area.
- **National brand awareness** — £800 can't buy it; it would vanish.
- **Expensive directories / trade-mag display ads** — only if one can be measured and tied to signups.
- **New creative for ads** when a proven organic video can be boosted instead.
- **A second region** before the first one is working.
